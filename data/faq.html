Q: What should I include in my complaint?

A: Describe the issue clearly, include error messages, and steps to reproduce.

Q: Can I attach files?

A: Yes, screenshots or documents help speed up resolution.

Q: How long does it take?

A: IT reviews complaints in order of urgency.

Q: What happens next?

A: Your complaint is logged and assigned for review.

Q: Do I need to submit multiple times?

A: No, one detailed report is enough.

Q: Any tips for faster help?

A: Attach a screenshot and mention your department.

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Access is restricted to IT staff.

Q: What if I uploaded the wrong file?

A: Submit a follow-up complaint with corrected info.

Q: Can I edit a complaint?

A: Not yet. Contact IT if you need to update details.

Q: What issues are urgent?

A: System outages, security risks, and work stoppages.

Q: Will I be notified?

A: IT will reach out if more information is needed.
